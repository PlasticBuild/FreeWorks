9. Keys and mouse

These are the defaults the add-on sets, taken from the keys it ships. Change any of them in Customize, Keyboard (chapter 8). A key not on this page is not set by the add-on: FreeCAD's own key for it stays, except where the add-on has taken that letter for a key below (A and D in a sketch or panel, for example, no longer start FreeCAD's constraint commands). Press S to see the shortcut bar, W to search for any command by name.

Mouse gestures

Hold the right button and flick in a direction; let go over a command to run it, or in the middle to cancel. The ring depends on where you are: a part, an assembly, a drawing or a sketch (an assembly with a part being edited uses the part's ring). Four directions is the default.

DirectionPartAssemblySketchDrawing
UpTop viewTop viewSmart DimensionSmart Dimension
RightRight viewRight viewCircleDetail View
DownBottom viewBottom viewCorner RectangleNote
LeftLeft viewLeft viewLineHole Callout

In a property panel, up is OK and down is Cancel. With eight directions the corners are added:

DirectionPart and assemblySketchDrawing
Up-RightTrimetricExit SketchCenter Mark
Down-RightZoom to AreaSketch FilletCenterline
Down-LeftBack viewTrim EntitiesSection View
Up-LeftFront viewNormal ToModel View

In Customize, Mouse Gestures you can choose 2 (vertical or horizontal), 3, 4, 8 or 12 gestures and change the command of every direction, for each of the four rings. With two gestures a part has OK and Cancel, an assembly Mate and Measure, a sketch Smart Dimension and Exit Sketch, and a drawing Smart Dimension and Note; three gestures add the shortcut bar at the top. Twelve gestures put four more commands between the eight: View Orientation, Zoom to Fit, Pan and Redo in a part or assembly; Construction Geometry, Offset Entities, Spline and Slot in a sketch; Ordinate Dimension, Corner Rectangle, Balloon and Bill of Materials in a drawing. A right-click without a drag still opens the menu; Tools, Mouse Gestures switches gestures off and on.

The S key: shortcut bar

Press S to open a bar of commands at the pointer. It changes with what you are doing, and every bar ends with a Search All Commands box. A button with a small arrow opens a flyout of related commands.

WhereButtons
PartSelect (Box Select, Lasso Select, Magnified Selection, Box Element Select, Select All), Extruded Boss/Base (Revolved, Swept, Lofted, Boundary), Extruded Cut (Revolved, Swept, Lofted, Boundary), Fillet (Chamfer), Reference Geometry (Plane, Axis, Coordinate System, Point, Center of Mass, Bounding Box), Hole (Advanced Hole, Thread)
AssemblySelect, Insert Components (New Part), Mate, Edit Component, Show Hidden Components
SketchSelect, Smart Dimension (with the other dimensions), Exit Sketch, Line (Centerline, Midpoint Line), Corner Rectangle, Slot, Circle, Center Arc, Sketch Fillet (Chamfer), Spline, Trim (Extend), Convert Entities (Silhouette Entities, Intersection Curve), Offset Entities

The flyout lists are shortened here; the bar shows the complete ones.

Macros

The Macro toolbar (View, Toolbars, Macro) has Run, Record, Stop, New and Edit. Tools, Macro, Run... is a file box; Stop opens the Record box, or says "No macro is being recorded.". A macro runs with the console names plus App and Gui, and the macro folder is on its path. To give a macro its own button and key, see Customize, Commands, Macro in chapter 8.
