Drawing: Drawing

Standard 3 View

Adds a part's or assembly's Front, Top and Right views to the sheet.

How to use it

  • Pick a part or assembly from the list, or Browse... for one.
  • OK puts its Front, Top and Right views on the sheet, lined up.

Where: the Drawing tab of the drawing ribbon.

More: 6. Drawings.

Model View

Adds one view of a part or assembly in the orientation you pick, then views projected from it.

How to use it

  • Pick the part or assembly in the list of open documents, or Browse... for a file, then click Next .
  • Pick a view in Orientation (Front, Top, Isometric and so on). Create multiple views places several at once.
  • Set the Display Style and the Scale, then click on the sheet where the view goes.
  • With Auto-start projected view on, move the pointer away from the view and click to add projected views of it; OK when you have enough.

Where: the Drawing tab of the drawing ribbon.

More: 8. Make a drawing.

Projected View

Adds a view projected from the picked view: click to its right, left, above or below.

How to use it

  • Click the view to project from (or pick it first, then the command).
  • Move the pointer to the side, above or below it: the preview shows the view from that direction.
  • Click to place it. It lines up with its parent and uses the parent's scale and display style until you change them in the PropertyManager.

Where: the Drawing tab of the drawing ribbon.

More: 6. Drawings.

How to use it

  • Click where the view goes.

Where: the Drawing tab of the drawing ribbon.

More: 6. Drawings.

Section View

Cuts a view along a line you sketch and adds the section as a new view.

How to use it

  • Pick the cutting line type: Vertical, Horizontal, Auxiliary or Aligned (or a Half Section shape).
  • Click in a view to place the cutting line; it snaps to centres and edges. A pop-up offers offsets (arc, single or notch) before you go on.
  • Click OK in the pop-up, then click where the section view goes. Flip Direction turns the arrows round.
  • Set the hatch, Partial section, Slice section or Section depth in the PropertyManager, then OK.

Where: the Drawing tab of the drawing ribbon.

More: In the 3D view.

Hole Callout

A callout with a hole's diameter and depth: click the hole's edge, then where the note goes.

How to use it

  • Click the edge of a hole in a view, then click where the callout goes.
  • The callout lists the hole's size, depth and thread for a Hole Wizard hole, and the diameter for other round edges. Once it is placed, Callout variables... puts a value (depth, counterbore, thread...) into its text.
  • Change the tolerance, precision and text in the PropertyManager. The tool stays on for the next hole: OK or Esc ends it.

Where: the Annotation tab of the drawing ribbon.

Note

A text note on the sheet, with or without a leader.

How to use it

  • Pick a leader type in Leader. No Leader gives plain text; Auto Leader adds one only when you start on a view.
  • With a leader, click where it points, then where the note goes; without one, just click where the note goes.
  • Type the text in the Text box. Text Format sets the font, and adds symbols, geometric tolerances, hole callouts or links to properties.
  • The tool stays on for the next note: OK or Esc ends it.

Where: the Annotation tab of the drawing ribbon.

  • The tool stays on: click the next part, then OK. Auto Balloon does every part of a view at once.
